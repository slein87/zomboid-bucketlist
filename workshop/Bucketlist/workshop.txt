description=[h1]Bucketlist[/h1]
description=A wishlist for items you do not want to leave behind while looting. Build 42.21 and later.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Right-click[/b] an item in the inventory, loot, hotbar or hand slots: "Add to Bucketlist" or "Remove from Bucketlist".
description=[*][b]Bucketlist window[/b] (sidebar button or hotkey): icon, name, carried and wanted amount, sources and note per entry. Change the wanted amount, edit notes, delete entries, hide done entries, open the recipe of an entry.
description=[*][b]Highlight[/b]: listed items that are still wanted get a coloured frame in the inventory and loot windows (colour in the mod options).
description=[*][b]Tooltip[/b]: carried and wanted amount, sources and note under the item tooltip.
description=[*][b]Crafting window[/b]: "Missing ingredients to Bucketlist" puts every missing input of the selected recipe on the list. Right-click a single ingredient or tool to add or remove just that one. Inputs with alternatives become one entry, tools are needed once.
description=[*]The list belongs to your character and is saved with it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Controls[/h2]
description=Hotkey: K. Rebind it in Options, Mods, Bucketlist.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=No server code, but the server must list the mod in Mods= and WorkshopItems=, and everybody needs the same version. Not tested in multiplayer yet.
